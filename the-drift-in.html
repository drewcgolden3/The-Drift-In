Welcome In

Right on the Driftway, overlooking the marsh.

The Drift In sits at Widow's Walk Golf Course, but you don't need to play a round to pull up a chair. Golfers grab a drink and a bite mid-course; everyone else comes for the sun-drenched patio, the herring brook view, and a relaxed New England menu.

We're open to the public for lunch and dinner all week — plus breakfast every Sunday at 10am. The course closes for the season; we don't.
